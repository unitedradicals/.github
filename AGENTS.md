# AGENTS.md: unitedradicals/.github

This public repo holds the **GitHub organization profile** of United Radicals, a worker-owned co-op of software engineers displaced by AI. `profile/README.md` is shown at the top of https://github.com/unitedradicals.

- Keep it short: who we are, how it works, where to start. Visitors skim it.
- Facts such as the voting rules and revenue splits come from the charter repo (`unitedradicals/charter`). Check the numbers there before changing them here.
- Links: Discord `https://unitedradicals.com/discord`, join `https://unitedradicals.com/#join`, introductions `https://github.com/unitedradicals/charter/discussions/1`.
- Voice: direct, warm, plain words, no hype, sentence case (see `docs/brand.md` in the charter repo).
- Every push to `main` is public right away.

Other repos: `unitedradicals/charter` (public: founding docs, Discussions), `unitedradicals/website` (private: unitedradicals.com), `unitedradicals/ops` (private: stewards' working files).
