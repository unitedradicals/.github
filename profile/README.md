# United √adicals

**They deprecated us. We united.**

We're a worker-owned co-op of software engineers who lost their jobs to AI. Members propose products, vote on what to build, build them in small teams, and share what they earn. No investors, no bosses.

### How it works
1. **Propose:** any member can post an idea for a product.
2. **Back:** an idea needs 5 members who commit real hours before it goes to a vote.
3. **Vote:** one member, one base vote, plus a capped bonus for contribution (max 2×).
4. **Build and share:** small teams build it, using AI as a tool. Revenue goes to the people who built it, all members, and a shared treasury.
